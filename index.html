Ingredient 01

Shankh Bhasma

Turbinella pyrum (Conch Shell)
Classical Ayurvedic Marine Mineral
Role Mineral Gastroprotective Component

Key Scientific Highlights

  • Calcium-Rich Mineral
  • Acid Neutralization Rationale
  • Traditional Pitta-Pacifying Support

Classical Ayurvedic Perspective

Shankh Bhasma is a marine-derived mineral preparation described in the classical texts of Rasashastra and classified under Sudha Varga (calcium-rich substances). Traditionally, it has been incorporated into Ayurvedic formulations intended for disorders associated with Amlapitta, Agnimandya, Parinama Shoola, and other digestive complaints.

Following Shodhana (purification) and Marana (incineration), the conch shell is transformed into a fine, bioavailable Bhasma suitable for internal administration according to classical Ayurvedic pharmaceutics.

Modern Scientific Perspective

Shankh Bhasma is prepared from purified conch shell, which is composed predominantly of calcium carbonate (CaCO₃). During the traditional pharmaceutical process, the naturally occurring aragonite crystalline form undergoes transformation toward calcite, accompanied by removal of the organic matrix and changes in physicochemical characteristics. These transformations have been demonstrated using techniques such as X-ray diffraction (XRD), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), FTIR, thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), and ICP-OES.

Analytical studies of pharmacopeial-quality Shankh Bhasma have reported:

  • Approximately 38–40% elemental calcium, consistent with the standards of the Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India.
  • Heavy metal levels within prescribed pharmacopoeial limits when manufactured according to validated procedures.
  • Fine particle size and characteristic physicochemical properties suitable for standardized quality assessment.
Scientific Rationale

Calcium carbonate has long been used in conventional medicine as an acid-neutralizing agent. Its ability to react with gastric hydrochloric acid provides the scientific rationale for its historical use in antacid preparations, although calcium-containing antacids also have pharmacological considerations such as the potential for acid rebound under some conditions.

Within Ayurveda, Shankh Bhasma has traditionally been selected for formulations intended to support digestive balance. Modern analytical research has focused primarily on its pharmaceutical standardization and physicochemical characterization, while further clinical research is needed to better understand product-specific effects.

Shankh Bhasma was selected because it:

Rather than functioning as a standalone solution, Shankh Bhasma is incorporated as one component of a carefully designed formulation intended to work through complementary traditional and pharmaceutical principles.

Ingredient 02

Laghu Sutshekhar Ras

AFI Classical Polyherbo-Mineral Formulation

Key Scientific Highlights

  • Official AFI Formulation
  • Shuddha Gairika + Shunthi + Nagavalli Bhavana
  • Classical Amlapitta Formulation
  • Multi-Component Digestive Strategy

Overview

A precisely designed classical Ayurvedic formulation combining purified mineral and botanical components to support digestive balance through complementary traditional principles.

Classical Ayurvedic Perspective

Laghu Sutshekhar Ras is an official formulation described in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI) and classical Rasashastra literature. It has traditionally been used in the management of Amlapitta, Pitta Prakopa, Parinama Shoola, and other disorders associated with aggravated Pitta and disturbed digestion.

Unlike formulations that rely on numerous ingredients, Laghu Sutshekhar Ras follows a focused formulation philosophy in which each component contributes a distinct functional role.

Composition & Scientific Rationale
Shuddha Gairika (Purified Red Ochre)

Traditional Role

Shuddha Gairika is described as a Pitta-Shamaka mineral traditionally indicated in conditions associated with burning sensation (Daha), Amlapitta, and other Pitta-predominant disorders.

Scientific Perspective

Purified Gairika consists predominantly of iron oxide (Fe₂O₃). Modern investigations have focused on its pharmaceutical purification, physicochemical characterization, and its contribution within classical formulations rather than as a standalone therapeutic agent. Experimental work has also explored antioxidant and antacid-related properties of Laghu Sutshekhar Ras as a formulation.

Role in H-Reflux™

Provides the principal mineral foundation of the classical formulation and supports the traditional Pitta-balancing philosophy.

Shunthi (Zingiber officinale)

Traditional Role

Shunthi is one of Ayurveda’s most respected digestive herbs, traditionally described as Deepana and Pachana, supporting digestive efficiency while helping maintain balanced Agni.

Scientific Perspective

Dry ginger contains bioactive constituents including gingerols, shogaols, and zingerone, which have been extensively investigated for digestive support, antioxidant activity, anti-inflammatory effects, and gastrointestinal motility. Experimental studies have also explored its role in gastric protection and digestive comfort. (These findings relate to the ingredient itself, not specifically to the finished H-Reflux™ formulation.)

Role in H-Reflux™

Introduces the botanical digestive component, complementing the mineral phase and supporting the formulation’s balanced digestive philosophy.

Nagavalli Patra Swarasa (Piper betle Leaf Juice)

Traditional Role

Fresh juice of Nagavalli leaves is used as the Bhavana Dravya (levigation medium) during pharmaceutical preparation. In Ayurveda, Bhavana is intended to enhance integration of the formulation and impart specific therapeutic qualities.

Scientific Perspective

Piper betle leaves contain naturally occurring phenolic compounds and essential oils, including hydroxychavicol, eugenol, and chavibetol, which have been investigated for antioxidant and antimicrobial activities. In Laghu Sutshekhar Ras, Nagavalli serves primarily as the traditional pharmaceutical processing medium, contributing to the classical preparation process rather than functioning as a major quantitative ingredient.

Role in H-Reflux™

Maintains authenticity of the AFI preparation process and reflects Herbionix’s commitment to classical Ayurvedic pharmaceutical principles.

Formulation Philosophy

Rather than relying on a single active ingredient, Laghu Sutshekhar Ras follows Ayurveda’s principle of multi-component formulation synergy. The formulation integrates:

  • Mineral Support — Shuddha Gairika
  • Digestive Botanical — Shunthi
  • Traditional Pharmaceutical Processing — Nagavalli Swarasa Bhavana

Together these form a balanced Ayurvedic formulation designed to support digestive physiology through complementary classical principles.

Why Herbionix Selected Laghu Sutshekhar Ras

H-Reflux™ was designed by integrating carefully selected classical and botanical components with modern pharmaceutical development.

Laghu Sutshekhar Ras was chosen because it:

  • Is an official formulation recognized by the Ayurvedic Formulary of India.

  • Combines a purified mineral with a well-studied digestive botanical.

  • Complements the broader gastroprotective formulation strategy of H-Reflux™.

Classical Ayurvedic Perspective

Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) is one of Ayurveda’s most important Rasayana herbs and is extensively described in the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, Ashtanga Hridaya, and Bhavaprakasha Nighantu.

Traditionally, it is indicated in conditions associated with Pitta aggravation, Amlapitta, burning sensation (Daha), and gastric irritation. Due to its Sheeta Virya (cooling potency) and nourishing (Brimhana) properties, Shatavari has long been used to support digestive comfort and maintain healthy gastric function.

Modern Scientific Perspective

Shatavari is a phytochemically rich medicinal plant widely investigated for its role in gastrointestinal health and tissue protection. Its root contains several naturally occurring bioactive compounds, including:

  • Shatavarins (Steroidal Saponins)
  • Sarsasapogenin
  • Flavonoids
  • Polyphenols
  • Mucilage

These constituents have been investigated in experimental studies for their gastroprotective, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and mucosal protective properties. Preclinical research has also explored its potential role in protecting the gastric mucosa against experimentally induced injury.

Scientific Insights

Modern research has investigated Shatavari for its potential to:

  • Act as a natural cooling (Pitta-Shamaka) botanical
  • Support gastric mucosal health
  • Help maintain the stomach’s protective mucus layer
  • Provide antioxidant support against oxidative stress
  • Support tissue healing and digestive comfort

These findings relate to the ingredient itself and should not be interpreted as established clinical effects of the finished H-Reflux™ formulation.

IVQuality & Compliance

Quality at Herbionix is built into every stage, not checked in at the end. From the first supplier we approve to the last stability reading on a shelf-life study, each step is governed by written procedures, verified by testing, and recorded in a way that can be traced back.

GMP Manufacturing

Production is designed around Good Manufacturing Practice principles, so every tablet is made in a controlled, documented and repeatable environment.

  • Controlled and documented production environment
  • Qualified, calibrated equipment
  • Trained personnel and hygiene protocols

COA System

A Certificate of Analysis records the tested parameters of each batch against approved specifications, for incoming materials and for the finished product.

  • Batch-specific test results
  • Results compared against specification
  • Retained with the batch record

SOP-Driven Manufacturing

Every critical step follows a written, approved Standard Operating Procedure, so each batch is made the same way, every time.

  • Approved, version-controlled SOPs
  • Defined process parameters and in-process checks
  • Deviation and change-control handling

Batch Traceability

Each batch carries a unique number that links raw materials, processing records, test results and dispatch, from start to finish.

  • Unique batch numbering
  • Raw material to finished goods linkage
  • Records that support investigation and recall readiness

Raw Material Qualification

Botanical, extract and mineral inputs are identified and tested before they are approved for use in any batch.

  • Botanical identity and pharmacognostic checks
  • Heavy metal and microbial screening
  • Released for use only after QC approval

Vendor Qualification

Suppliers are assessed and approved before they supply Herbionix, and are reviewed on an ongoing basis.

  • Supplier assessment and approval
  • Authenticated, traceable sourcing
  • Periodic performance review

Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance oversees the system itself: reviewing records, approving release decisions and making sure procedures are actually followed.

  • Document and batch record review
  • Batch release decisions
  • Deviation, change control and corrective actions

Quality Control

Laboratory testing verifies materials and products against specification at each stage, from incoming goods to finished tablets.

  • Identity and assay testing
  • Heavy metal and microbial limits
  • In-process and finished product testing

Stability Monitoring

Stability studies track how the product holds up over time under defined storage conditions, supporting shelf-life and storage recommendations.

  • Defined storage conditions
  • Scheduled testing intervals
  • Stability reports retained and reviewed

Documentation System

Accurate, controlled and retrievable records for every step. If it is not documented, it is not considered done.

  • Batch manufacturing records
  • COAs, testing and stability reports
  • Controlled storage and retention of records
